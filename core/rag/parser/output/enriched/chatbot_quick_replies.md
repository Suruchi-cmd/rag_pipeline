AeroSports Scarborough offers a variety of activities including wall-to-wall trampolines, a ninja warrior course, a ninja maze, rock climbing walls, dodgeball, slam basketball, a 360 bicycle ride, giant slides, a jump tower, and go karting. Everything except go karting is included in your jump pass.

A ninety-minute Premium Jump Pass costs twenty-four dollars and ninety cents, while a one hundred and twenty-minute VIP Jump Pass costs twenty-nine dollars and ninety cents. The All Day Pass is priced at thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents. AeroSports grip socks are required for participation and cost three dollars and ninety-nine cents but are reusable. All prices mentioned are plus tax. Go karting is available separately starting at nine dollars and ninety cents.

AeroSports Scarborough welcomes guests of all ages as long as they can walk. Children under two years old can enter free with a paying adult. Additionally, there is Toddler Time on Saturday mornings from nine to ten o'clock in the morning exclusively for children five years old and under, which costs fifteen dollars and ninety cents per child, and one parent jumps free.

For birthday parties, AeroSports Scarborough offers three packages starting at thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper with a minimum of ten kids. All packages include pizza, drinks, access to a private party room, a glow t-shirt for the birthday child, and digital invitations. The Ultimate Package also includes go karting, a free thirty-day pass, and free socks for everyone.

AeroSports Scarborough is located at one thousand one hundred and twenty Birchmount Road in Scarborough, Ontario, M1K 5G4. You can find it on Google Maps by searching AeroSports Scarborough. The phone number to contact them is two eight nine dash four five four dash five five five five.

Safety is a top priority at AeroSports Scarborough. They have trained staff and park rules posted throughout the facility. There is a three hundred pound weight limit on trampolines, and all jumpers need a signed waiver. Children under ten years old must be accompanied by an adult. Spectators are always free to watch, and there is comfortable seating along with complimentary Wi-Fi available for parents.
