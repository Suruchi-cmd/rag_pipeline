Welcome to AeroSports Scarborough! We offer a variety of jumping passes that cater to different age groups and preferences, ensuring everyone has an exciting time at our trampoline attractions. Our options include the Premium Jump Pass, VIP Jump Pass, Ultimate Jump Pass, and All Day Pass. Each pass provides access to various activities within the park, with specific inclusions based on your choice.

For children aged six years and older, we offer a Premium Jump Pass priced at twenty-four dollars and ninety cents for ninety minutes. This pass grants access to all our attractions except Go Karting, Laser Tag, Bazooka Ball, and Mini Golf. If you're looking for more time, the VIP Jump Pass is available for twenty-nine dollars and ninety cents, offering one hundred and twenty minutes of fun with the same inclusions as the Premium Jump Pass.

Younger children between two to five years old can also enjoy our jumping passes. They have access to the same Premium Jump Pass at twenty-four dollars and ninety cents for ninety minutes or the VIP Jump Pass at twenty-nine dollars and ninety cents for one hundred and twenty minutes, with identical exclusions of Go Karting, Laser Tag, Bazooka Ball, and Mini Golf.

For those seeking an extended experience, our Ultimate Jump Pass is perfect. Priced at forty-nine dollars and ninety cents, it offers one hundred and eighty minutes of access to all core attractions. Additionally, you can choose between Go Kart or Mini Golf and Laser Tag or Bazooka Ball as extra activities included in your pass.

For the littlest jumpers under two years old, we offer free entry with any pass type, provided they are accompanied by a paying adult. This allows them to enjoy the same duration of fun as their accompanying adult.

Lastly, our All Day Pass is available for thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents, granting unlimited access throughout the day to all attractions. It's an excellent choice if you want to jump all day long without any time restrictions.

In addition to these passes, we require all jumpers to wear Aero Socks, which are priced at three dollars and ninety-nine cents. These socks are reusable and must be worn for safety on all park attractions. You can bring them back for future visits, making it a convenient option for regular guests.

If you have any questions or need further assistance with booking your pass, feel free to ask!
