## Jump Prices

### Open Jump Passes

For children aged six and older, the Premium Jump Pass costs twenty-four dollars and ninety cents and provides ninety minutes of access to all attractions except Go Karting, Laser Tag, Bazooka Ball, and Mini Golf. The VIP Jump Pass for the same age group is priced at twenty-nine dollars and ninety cents and offers one hundred and twenty minutes of access with the same exclusions.

Children aged two to five can also purchase a Premium Jump Pass for twenty-four dollars and ninety cents, granting them ninety minutes of access to all attractions except Go Karting, Laser Tag, Bazooka Ball, and Mini Golf. Alternatively, they can opt for the VIP Jump Pass at twenty-nine dollars and ninety cents, which provides one hundred and twenty minutes of access with the same exclusions.

For those aged six and older, the Ultimate Jump Pass is available for forty-nine dollars and ninety cents, offering one hundred and eighty minutes of access to all core attractions. This pass allows the choice between Go Karting or Mini Golf and Laser Tag or Bazooka Ball as additional activities.

Children under two years old can enjoy any pass for free, provided they are accompanied by a paying adult. The duration is the same as that of their accompanying parent's pass.

The All Day Pass is available to all ages at thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents. It offers unlimited access to attractions throughout the entire day, allowing jumpers to enjoy all park activities without time restrictions.

### Required Gear

Aero Socks are required for all jumpers on all park attractions, regardless of age. These reusable socks cost three dollars and ninety-nine cents and must be brought back for future visits.
