## Jump Prices

### Open Jump Passes

AeroSports Scarborough offers various open jump passes tailored to different age groups and preferences.

- **Premium Jump Pass**: For guests aged six years and older, the Premium Jump Pass costs twenty-four dollars and ninety cents for ninety minutes. This pass grants access to all attractions except Go Karting, Laser Tag, Bazooka Ball, and Mini Golf.
  
- **VIP Jump Pass**: Also available for guests aged six years and older, the VIP Jump Pass is priced at twenty-nine dollars and ninety cents for a duration of one hundred and twenty minutes. Similar to the Premium Jump Pass, it includes access to all attractions except Go Karting, Laser Tag, Bazooka Ball, and Mini Golf.

- **Premium Jump Pass for Ages 2 to 5**: Children aged two to five can enjoy the same benefits as older guests with this pass, which costs twenty-four dollars and ninety cents for ninety minutes. It includes access to all attractions except Go Karting, Laser Tag, Bazooka Ball, and Mini Golf.

- **VIP Jump Pass for Ages 2 to 5**: This pass is available for children aged two to five at a cost of twenty-nine dollars and ninety cents for one hundred and twenty minutes. It provides the same access as the VIP Jump Pass for older guests.

- **Ultimate Jump Pass**: Designed for guests aged six years and older, the Ultimate Jump Pass costs forty-nine dollars and ninety cents for one hundred and eighty minutes. This pass includes access to all core attractions, with the option to choose between Go Karting or Mini Golf and Laser Tag or Bazooka Ball.

- **Passes for Children Under 2**: Any jump pass is free for children under two years old, provided they are accompanied by a paying adult. The duration matches that of the accompanying adult's pass.

- **All Day Pass**: Available to all guests, the All Day Jump Pass costs thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents and allows unlimited access to attractions throughout the day.

### Required Gear

For safety and convenience, AeroSports Scarborough requires all jumpers to wear Aero Socks. These socks are priced at three dollars and ninety-nine cents and are reusable. Guests must bring them back for future visits to enjoy this requirement across all park attractions.
