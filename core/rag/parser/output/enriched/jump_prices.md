# Jump Prices

This page covers the various pricing options available for jumping activities at AeroSports Scarborough, including details about different pass types and their respective durations, prices, and inclusions.

## Open Jump

The Premium Jump Pass is available for ages 6+ and costs $24.90 for a duration of 90 minutes. It includes access to all attractions except Go Karting, Laser tag, bazooka ball, and mini golf. For the same age group, the VIP Jump Pass costs $29.90 and lasts 120 minutes, also excluding Go Karting, Laser tag, bazooka ball, and mini golf.

For children aged 2 to 5, the Premium Jump Pass is priced at $24.90 for a duration of 90 minutes, with access to all attractions except Go Karting, Laser tag, bazooka ball, and mini golf. The VIP Jump Pass for this age group costs $29.90 and lasts 120 minutes, excluding the same attractions.

The Ultimate Jump Pass is available for ages 6+ at a price of $49.9 for 180 minutes. It includes access to all core attractions, allowing you to choose either mini golf and bazooka ball or go karting and laser tag.

Children under 2 can use any pass for free, with the duration being the same as that of their accompanying paying adult. A paying adult is required to accompany the child.

The All Day Pass costs $39.90 and offers unlimited access to attractions throughout the day, allowing you to jump all day long.

## Required Gear

Aero Socks are priced at $3.99 and are reusable. They are required for ALL jumpers on all park attractions and should be brought back for future visits.
