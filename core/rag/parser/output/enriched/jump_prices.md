The Premium and VIP pass options are available for different age groups and include various features. 

For individuals aged six years or older, the Premium Jump Pass costs twenty-four dollars and ninety cents and lasts ninety minutes. This pass grants access to all attractions except Go Karting, Laser tag, Bazooka ball, and Mini golf.

 Alternatively, the VIP Jump Pass for the same age group is priced at twenty-nine dollars and ninety cents and provides one hundred and twenty minutes of access with the same exclusions.

For children aged two to five years, the Premium Jump Pass also costs twenty-four dollars and ninety cents and lasts ninety minutes, offering similar access as the adult version. The VIP Jump Pass for this age group is priced at twenty-nine dollars and ninety cents and provides one hundred and twenty minutes of access with the same exclusions.

For individuals aged six years or older, there is an Ultimate Jump Pass available for forty-nine dollars and ninety cents, which lasts one hundred and eighty minutes. This pass includes access to all core attractions, allowing the choice between Go Karting or Mini golf and Laser tag or Bazooka ball.

Children under two years old can use any pass for free, but a paying adult must accompany them, sharing the same duration as the accompanying parent's pass.

The All Day Pass is available for all ages at thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents, offering unlimited access to attractions throughout the entire day.

Regarding required gear, Aero Socks are priced at three dollars and ninety-nine cents. These socks are reusable and mandatory for all jumpers on all park attractions. Visitors are encouraged to bring them back for future visits.
