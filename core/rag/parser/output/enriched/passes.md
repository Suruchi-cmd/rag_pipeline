The All Day Pass is valid until the end of the day and costs thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents. It allows unlimited jump time throughout the entire day.

The Thirty-Day Pass remains valid for thirty days from the date of purchase, with a price of forty-nine dollars and ninety cents plus tax. This pass provides sixty minutes of jump time per visit.

The Ninety-Day Pass is valid for ninety days starting from the purchase date and costs one hundred and forty-nine dollars and ninety cents plus tax. It also offers sixty minutes of jump time per visit.

The Annual Pass has a validity period of three hundred and sixty-five days from the purchase date, with a price tag of three hundred and sixty-four dollars and ninety cents plus tax. Like the other passes, it provides sixty minutes of jump time per visit.
