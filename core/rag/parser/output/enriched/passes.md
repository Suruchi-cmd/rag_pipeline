# Passes

At AeroSports Scarborough, we offer a variety of passes to suit different visiting preferences and durations. This page covers the details of our All Day Pass, 30 Day Pass, 90 Day Pass, and Annual Pass, including their validity periods, prices, and jump times.

## All Day Pass

- **Validity:** Valid by end of the day
- **Price:** $39.90
- **Jump Time:** All day

The All Day Pass is valid until the end of the day it is purchased for a price of $39.90, allowing unlimited jump time throughout that day.

## 30 Day Pass

- **Validity:** Valid 30 days from purchase
- **Price:** $49.90+tax
- **Jump Time:** 60 Minutes per visit

The 30 Day Pass is valid for 30 days from the date of purchase and costs $49.90 plus tax, providing 60 minutes of jump time per visit.

## 90 Day Pass

- **Validity:** Valid 90 days from purchase
- **Price:** $149.90+tax
- **Jump Time:** 60 Minutes per visit

The 90 Day Pass is valid for 90 days from the date of purchase and costs $149.90 plus tax, offering 60 minutes of jump time per visit.

## Annual Pass

- **Validity:** Valid 365 days from purchase
- **Price:** $364.90+tax
- **Jump Time:** 60 Minutes per visit

The Annual Pass is valid for 365 days from the date of purchase and costs $364.90 plus tax, allowing 60 minutes of jump time per visit.
