# Birthday Parties

At AeroSports Scarborough, we offer a variety of birthday party packages designed to provide fun and memorable experiences for children and their friends. This page covers the details of our Premium, VIP, and Ultimate Party packages, including pricing, included amenities, and available add-ons.

## Premium Party

The Premium Party is priced at $39.90 per jumper with a minimum requirement of 10 jumpers, resulting in a base price of $399. It includes 75 minutes on the trampoline courts followed by 45 minutes in a private party room that accommodates 25-30 people. Food included consists of one large pizza for every five jumpers and one juice box per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free 60-minute jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a free 60-minute add-on pass. Go karting is not included but can be added on for $9.90 per person. There are no discounts for socks or physical invitations, and bringing outside food incurs a $50 fee. The total party duration is approximately two hours.

## VIP Party

The VIP Party costs $49.90 per jumper with the same minimum of 10 jumpers, leading to a base price of $499. This package includes 90 minutes on the trampoline courts followed by 60 minutes in a private party room for 25-30 people. Food included consists of one large pizza for every five jumpers and both a juice box and a water bottle per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free 60-minute jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a free 60-minute add-on pass. Socks are available at a 50% discount, costing $2.00 instead of the regular price of $3.99. Go karting is not included but can be added on for $9.90 per person. There are no physical invitations included, and outside food requires a $50 fee. The total party duration is approximately two and a half hours.

## Ultimate Party

The Ultimate Party is priced at $59.90 per jumper with the same minimum of 10 jumpers, resulting in a base price of $599. This package includes 120 minutes on the trampoline courts followed by 60 minutes in a private party room for 25-30 people. Food included consists of one large pizza for every five jumpers and either a juice box or pop along with a water bottle per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital and physical invitations, and a free 60-minute jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a free 60-minute add-on pass. Socks are provided for free to all jumpers. Go karting is included at one race per jumper. The $50 outside food fee is waived. The total party duration is approximately three hours.

## All Packages

AeroSports Scarborough offers three birthday party packages: Premium, VIP, and Ultimate, priced at $39.90, $49.90, and $59.90 per jumper respectively. Each package requires a minimum of 10 jumpers and includes pizza, drinks, a private party room, a glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free 60-minute add-on pass for guest jumpers.

**What Upgrades From Premium To VIP:** Upgrading from the Premium to the VIP package adds 15 more minutes of jump time (90 vs. 75), 15 more minutes of room time (60 vs. 45), a water bottle in addition to the juice box, and 50% off socks ($2.00 instead of $3.99). The cost difference is $5 per jumper, which amounts to an additional $50 for 10 jumpers.

**What Upgrades From VIP To Ultimate:** Upgrading from the VIP to the Ultimate package adds 30 more minutes of jump time (120 vs. 90), includes go karting for every jumper, provides free socks for all jumpers, offers free physical invitations, gives a free 30-Day pass instead of a 60-minute one for the birthday child, waives the $50 outside food fee, and allows drinks to include pop as an option.

**Best Value:** The Ultimate Party is considered the best value due to go karting alone being worth $9.90 per jumper ($99 for 10 kids), free socks valued at $3.99 per jumper ($39.90 for 10), and the waived $50 outside food fee.

## Add-Ons

Birthday party add-ons available at AeroSports Scarborough include large pizzas (Cheese, Pepperoni, Halal Pepperoni, Vegetarian, or Hawaiian) priced at $25.00 each; juice boxes (10 boxes) for $30.00; soft drink cans (10 cans) for $20.00; and physical birthday invitations for $0.75 each. An outside food fee of $50.00 is waived for the Ultimate Party. A go kart race add-on costs $9.90 per person but is already included in the Ultimate Party. A late ordering fee within 24 hours is $20.00.

## Terms & Conditions

Birthday party terms and conditions at AeroSports Scarborough include free rescheduling up to one year from the booking date, a store gift card for cancellations with 14+ days notice, no refund for cancellations within 14 days, and package changes that must be made 14+ days in advance subject to availability. Waivers are required for all jumpers (under 18 must be signed by a parent/guardian and remain valid for one year). Room supplies such as plates, cups, and tablecloths are included. The jumping area is shared with other patrons, but the party room is private. Prohibited items include piñatas, confetti, or silly string, which incur a $40 cleaning charge if used. Food allergies/sensitivities should be advised in advance. All guests should arrive 20 minutes early for check-in. AeroSports is not responsible for lost or stolen items.
