AeroSports Scarborough offers three birthday party packages: Premium, VIP, and Ultimate. 

The Premium Party costs thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper with a minimum of ten jumpers required, at a base price of three hundred ninety-nine dollars. It includes seventy-five minutes on the trampoline courts followed by forty-five minutes in a private party room that fits twenty-five to thirty people. Food included is one large pizza for every five jumpers and one juice box per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free sixty-minute jump pass. All guest jumpers receive a free sixty-minute add-on pass. Go karting can be added on at nine dollars and ninety cents per person. There is no discount for socks, physical invitations are not included, and outside food requires a fifty-dollar fee. The total party duration is approximately two hours.

The VIP Party costs forty-four dollars and ninety cents per jumper with the same minimum of ten jumpers required, at a base price of four hundred forty-nine dollars. It includes ninety minutes on the trampoline courts followed by sixty minutes in a private party room that fits twenty-five to thirty people. Food included is one large pizza for every five jumpers, plus one juice box and one water bottle per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free sixty-minute jump pass. All guest jumpers receive a free sixty-minute add-on pass. Socks are fifty percent off at two dollars instead of three dollars and ninety-nine cents. Go karting can be added on for nine dollars and ninety cents per person. Physical invitations are not included, and outside food requires a fifty-dollar fee. The total party duration is approximately two and a half hours.

The Ultimate Party costs fifty-nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper with the same minimum of ten jumpers required, at a base price of five hundred ninety-nine dollars. It includes one hundred twenty minutes on the trampoline courts followed by sixty minutes in a private party room that fits twenty-five to thirty people. Food included is one large pizza for every five jumpers, plus one juice box or pop and one water bottle per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital and physical invitations, and a free thirty-day jump pass. All guest jumpers receive a free sixty-minute add-on pass. Socks are free for all jumpers. Go karting is included with one race per jumper. The fifty-dollar outside food fee is waived. The total party duration is approximately three hours.

Upgrading from Premium to VIP adds fifteen more minutes of jump time, fifteen more minutes of room time, a water bottle in addition to the juice box, and fifty percent off socks at two dollars instead of three dollars and ninety-nine cents. The cost difference is five dollars per jumper or fifty dollars for ten jumpers.

Upgrading from VIP to Ultimate adds thirty more minutes of jump time, includes go karting for every jumper, provides free socks for all jumpers, offers free physical invitations, gives a free thirty-day pass for the birthday child instead of sixty minutes, waives the fifty-dollar outside food fee, and allows drinks to include pop as an option. The cost difference is fifteen dollars per jumper or one hundred fifty dollars for ten jumpers.

The Ultimate Party offers the most value with go karting alone worth nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper, free socks at three dollars and ninety-nine cents per jumper, and a waived fifty-dollar outside food fee. This amounts to roughly one hundred eighty-nine dollars in extras for one hundred fifty dollars more than VIP.

Birthday party add-ons available include large pizzas (cheese, pepperoni, halal pepperoni, vegetarian, or Hawaiian) for twenty-five dollars each, juice boxes at thirty dollars for ten boxes, soft drink cans at twenty dollars for ten cans, physical birthday invitations at seventy-five cents each, and a go kart race add-on at nine dollars and ninety cents per person. The outside food fee is fifty dollars but is waived for the Ultimate Party. A late ordering fee within twenty-four hours is twenty dollars.

Terms and conditions include free rescheduling up to one year from the booking date. Cancellations with fourteen or more days notice receive a store gift card for the refund amount, while cancellations within fourteen days receive no refund. Package changes must be made fourteen or more days in advance and are subject to availability. Waivers are required for all jumpers, with those under eighteen needing parental or guardian signatures valid for one year. Room supplies like plates, cups, and tablecloths are included. The jumping area is shared, but the party room is private. Prohibited items include piñatas, confetti, or silly string, which incur a forty-dollar cleaning charge if used. Food allergies or sensitivities should be advised in advance. All guests should arrive twenty minutes early for check-in. AeroSports is not responsible for lost or stolen items.
