# Birthday Parties at AeroSports Scarborough

## Premium Party Details

The Premium Party package costs forty-nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper, with a minimum requirement of ten jumpers, resulting in a base price of three hundred ninety-nine dollars. This party includes seventy-five minutes on the trampoline courts followed by forty-five minutes in a private party room that accommodates twenty-five to thirty people. Food provided includes one large pizza for every five jumpers and one juice box per jumper. The birthday child receives complimentary items: a glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a sixty-minute free jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a sixty-minute add-on pass at no extra cost. Go karting is not included but can be added on for nine dollars and ninety cents per person. There are no discounts for socks, physical invitations are not provided, and bringing outside food incurs a fifty-dollar fee. The total party duration is approximately two hours.

## VIP Party Details

The VIP Party package costs forty-four dollars and ninety cents per jumper, with the same minimum requirement of ten jumpers, leading to a base price of four hundred forty-nine dollars. This package includes ninety minutes on the trampoline courts followed by sixty minutes in a private party room for twenty-five to thirty people. Food included consists of one large pizza for every five jumpers and both a juice box and a water bottle per jumper. The birthday child receives complimentary items: a glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a sixty-minute free jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a sixty-minute add-on pass at no extra cost. Socks are available at half price, costing two dollars instead of three dollars and ninety-nine cents. Go karting is not included but can be added on for nine dollars and ninety cents per person. Physical invitations are not provided, and bringing outside food incurs a fifty-dollar fee. The total party duration is approximately two and a half hours.

## Ultimate Party Details

The Ultimate Party package costs fifty-nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper, with the same minimum requirement of ten jumpers, resulting in a base price of five hundred ninety-nine dollars. This package includes one hundred twenty minutes on the trampoline courts followed by sixty minutes in a private party room for twenty-five to thirty people. Food provided consists of one large pizza for every five jumpers and either a juice box or pop along with a water bottle per jumper. The birthday child receives complimentary items: a glow t-shirt, both digital and physical invitations, and a free thirty-day jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a sixty-minute add-on pass at no extra cost. Socks are provided for free to all jumpers. Go karting is included with one race per jumper. The fifty-dollar fee for outside food is waived. The total party duration is approximately three hours, making this the most comprehensive package.

## Comparison of Packages

AeroSports Scarborough offers three birthday party packages: Premium at forty-nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper, VIP at forty-four dollars and ninety cents per jumper, and Ultimate at fifty-nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper. All packages require a minimum of ten jumpers and include pizza, drinks, a private party room, a glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free sixty-minute add-on pass for guest jumpers.

### Upgrades from Premium to VIP

Upgrading from the Premium to the VIP package adds fifteen more minutes on the trampoline courts (ninety versus seventy-five), fifteen additional minutes in the private party room (sixty versus forty-five), includes a water bottle along with the juice box, and offers socks at half price—two dollars instead of three dollars and ninety-nine cents. The cost difference is five dollars per jumper, or fifty dollars more for ten jumpers.

### Upgrades from VIP to Ultimate

Upgrading from the VIP to the Ultimate package adds thirty more minutes on the trampoline courts (one hundred twenty versus ninety), includes go karting for every jumper, provides free socks for all jumpers, offers both digital and physical invitations, upgrades the birthday child's pass to a thirty-day free jump pass instead of sixty minutes, waives the fifty-dollar outside food fee, and allows drinks to include pop as an option. The cost difference is fifteen dollars per jumper or one hundred fifty dollars more for ten jumpers.

### Best Value

The Ultimate Party offers the best value with go karting alone worth nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper (ninety-nine dollars for ten kids), free socks valued at three dollars and ninety-nine cents each (thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents for ten jumpers), and a waived fifty-dollar outside food fee. This amounts to roughly one hundred eighty-nine dollars in extras for an additional one hundred fifty dollars compared to the VIP package.

## Available Add-Ons

Birthday party add-ons available at AeroSports Scarborough include large pizzas—cheese, pepperoni, halal pepperoni, vegetarian, or Hawaiian—for twenty-five dollars each. Juice boxes (ten boxes) are priced at thirty dollars, soft drink cans (ten cans) cost twenty dollars, and physical birthday invitations are available for seventy-five cents each. An outside food fee of fifty dollars is waived for the Ultimate Party. A go kart race add-on costs nine dollars and ninety cents per person but is already included in the Ultimate Party package. A late ordering fee within twenty-four hours amounts to twenty dollars.

## Terms & Conditions

Birthday party terms and conditions at AeroSports Scarborough include free rescheduling up to one year from the booking date. Cancellations with fourteen or more days' notice receive a store gift card for the refund amount, while cancellations within fourteen days result in no refund. Package changes must be made fourteen or more days in advance and are subject to availability. Waivers are required for all jumpers; those under eighteen need parental or guardian signatures valid for one year. Room supplies like plates, cups, and tablecloths are included. The jumping area is shared with other patrons, but the party room remains private. Prohibited items include piñatas, confetti, or silly string, which incur a forty-dollar cleaning charge if used. Guests should inform of any food allergies or sensitivities in advance. All guests are advised to arrive twenty minutes early for check-in. AeroSports is not responsible for lost or stolen items.
