At AeroSports Scarborough, we offer three distinct birthday party packages: Premium, VIP, and Ultimate. Each package caters to groups of at least ten jumpers and includes a variety of amenities such as pizza, drinks, a private party room, a glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free sixty-minute add-on pass for guest jumpers.

The Premium Party is priced at thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper. This package requires a minimum of ten jumpers with a base price of three hundred and ninety-nine dollars. It includes seventy-five minutes on the trampoline courts followed by forty-five minutes in a private party room, which accommodates twenty-five to thirty people. Food included consists of one large pizza for every five jumpers and one juice box per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free sixty-minute jump pass, while all guest jumpers get a free sixty-minute add-on pass. Go karting is not included but can be added on at nine dollars and ninety cents per person. There are no discounts for socks, no physical invitations provided, and bringing outside food incurs a fifty-dollar fee. The total party duration is approximately two hours.

The VIP Party costs forty-four dollars and ninety cents per jumper with the same minimum requirement of ten jumpers and a base price of four hundred and forty-nine dollars. This package offers ninety minutes on the trampoline courts followed by sixty minutes in a private party room, also accommodating twenty-five to thirty people. Food includes one large pizza for every five jumpers, plus one juice box and one water bottle per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free sixty-minute jump pass, while all guest jumpers receive a free sixty-minute add-on pass. Socks are available at fifty percent off, costing two dollars instead of three dollars and ninety-nine cents. Go karting can be added on for nine dollars and ninety cents per person, with no physical invitations included, and outside food requires a fifty-dollar fee. The total party duration is approximately two and a half hours.

The Ultimate Party is priced at fifty-nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper, also requiring a minimum of ten jumpers with a base price of five hundred and ninety-nine dollars. This package includes one hundred and twenty minutes on the trampoline courts followed by sixty minutes in a private party room for twenty-five to thirty people. Food consists of one large pizza for every five jumpers, plus one juice box or pop and one water bottle per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital and physical invitations, and a free thirty-day jump pass. All guest jumpers receive a free sixty-minute add-on pass. Socks are provided for free to all jumpers, and go karting is included with one race per jumper. The fifty-dollar outside food fee is waived. The total party duration is approximately three hours.

When upgrading from the Premium Party to the VIP Party, you gain an additional fifteen minutes of jump time (ninety versus seventy-five), fifteen more minutes in the private room (sixty versus forty-five), a water bottle along with the juice box, and socks at fifty percent off (two dollars instead of three dollars and ninety-nine cents). The cost difference is five dollars per jumper, which amounts to fifty dollars for ten jumpers.

Upgrading from the VIP Party to the Ultimate Party adds thirty more minutes of jump time (one hundred and twenty versus ninety), includes go karting for every jumper, provides free socks for all jumpers, offers physical invitations, gives a birthday child a free thirty-day pass instead of sixty minutes, waives the fifty-dollar outside food fee, and allows drinks to include pop as an option. The cost difference is fifteen dollars per jumper, totaling one hundred and fifty dollars for ten jumpers.

The Ultimate Party offers the best value with go karting alone worth nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper (ninety-nine dollars for ten kids), free socks valued at three dollars and ninety-nine cents each (thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents for ten), and a waived fifty-dollar outside food fee. This equates to roughly one hundred and eighty-nine dollars in extras for one hundred and fifty dollars more than the VIP Party.

Additional add-ons available include large pizzas—cheese, pepperoni, halal pepperoni, vegetarian, or Hawaiian—for twenty-five dollars each; juice boxes (ten boxes) for thirty dollars; soft drink cans (ten cans) for twenty dollars; physical birthday invitations at seventy-five cents each. An outside food fee of fifty dollars is waived for the Ultimate Party. Go kart races can be added on for nine dollars and ninety cents per person, already included in the Ultimate Party. A late ordering fee within twenty-four hours is twenty dollars.

The terms and conditions for booking a birthday party include free rescheduling up to one year from the booking date. Cancellations with fourteen or more days' notice receive a store gift card for the refund amount; cancellations within fourteen days receive no refund. Package changes must be made fourteen or more days in advance, subject to availability. Waivers are required for all jumpers (under eighteen must be signed by a parent/guardian and remain valid for one year). Room supplies such as plates, cups, and tablecloths are included. The jumping area is shared with other patrons, but the party room is private. Prohibited items include piñatas, confetti, or silly string, which incur a forty-dollar cleaning charge if used. Food allergies or sensitivities should be advised in advance. All guests should arrive twenty minutes early for check-in. AeroSports Scarborough is not responsible for lost or stolen items.
