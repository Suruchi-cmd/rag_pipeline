# Birthday Parties at AeroSports Scarborough

AeroSports Scarborough offers three distinct birthday party packages: Premium, VIP, and Ultimate. Each package includes a minimum of ten jumpers and features amenities such as pizza, drinks, a private party room, a glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free sixty-minute add-on pass for guest jumpers.

## Premium Party

The Premium Party costs forty dollars and ninety cents per jumper with a base price of three hundred ninety-nine dollars. This package requires at least ten jumpers. It includes seventy-five minutes on the trampoline courts followed by forty-five minutes in a private party room, accommodating twenty-five to thirty people. Food included consists of one large pizza for every five jumpers and one juice box per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free sixty-minute jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a free sixty-minute add-on pass. Go-karting is not included but can be added on at nine dollars and ninety cents per person. There are no discounts for socks, physical invitations are not provided, and outside food incurs a fifty-dollar fee. The total party duration is approximately two hours.

## VIP Party

The VIP Party costs forty-four dollars and ninety cents per jumper with a base price of four hundred forty-nine dollars, also requiring at least ten jumpers. It includes ninety minutes on the trampoline courts followed by sixty minutes in a private party room for twenty-five to thirty people. Food included consists of one large pizza for every five jumpers, plus one juice box and one water bottle per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital invitations, and a free sixty-minute jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a free sixty-minute add-on pass. Socks are available at fifty percent off, costing two dollars instead of three dollars and ninety-nine cents. Go-karting is not included but can be added on for nine dollars and ninety cents per person. Physical invitations are not provided, and outside food incurs a fifty-dollar fee. The total party duration is approximately two and a half hours.

## Ultimate Party

The Ultimate Party costs fifty-nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper with a base price of five hundred ninety-nine dollars, requiring at least ten jumpers. It includes one hundred twenty minutes on the trampoline courts followed by sixty minutes in a private party room for twenty-five to thirty people. Food included consists of one large pizza for every five jumpers, plus one juice box or pop and one water bottle per jumper. The birthday child receives a free glow t-shirt, digital and physical invitations, and a free thirty-day jump pass. All guest jumpers also receive a free sixty-minute add-on pass. Socks are free for all jumpers. Go-karting is included with one race per jumper. The fifty-dollar outside food fee is waived. The total party duration is approximately three hours.

## Package Comparisons

### Upgrading from Premium to VIP

Upgrading from the Premium Party to the VIP Party adds fifteen more minutes of jump time, increasing from seventy-five to ninety minutes. It also includes an additional fifteen minutes in the private party room, extending from forty-five to sixty minutes. Additionally, a water bottle is included with each juice box, and socks are available at fifty percent off, costing two dollars instead of three dollars and ninety-nine cents. The cost difference between these packages is five dollars per jumper.

### Upgrading from VIP to Ultimate

Upgrading from the VIP Party to the Ultimate Party adds thirty more minutes of jump time, increasing from ninety to one hundred twenty minutes. Go-karting is included for every jumper in this package. Socks are free for all jumpers, and physical invitations are provided. The birthday child receives a free thirty-day jump pass instead of a sixty-minute pass. The fifty-dollar outside food fee is waived, and drinks include pop as an option. The cost difference between these packages is fifteen dollars per jumper.

### Best Value

The Ultimate Party offers the most value with go-karting alone worth nine dollars and ninety cents per jumper, totaling ninety-nine dollars for ten kids. Additionally, free socks are valued at three dollars and ninety-nine cents each, amounting to thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents for ten jumpers. The waived fifty-dollar outside food fee adds further value. These extras roughly total one hundred eighty-nine dollars in additional benefits for fifteen dollars more per jumper compared to the VIP Party.

## Add-Ons

Birthday party add-ons available at AeroSports Scarborough include large pizzas (cheese, pepperoni, halal pepperoni, vegetarian, or Hawaiian) for twenty-five dollars each. Juice boxes are available in packs of ten for thirty dollars. Soft drink cans come in packs of ten for twenty dollars. Physical birthday invitations cost seventy-five cents each. An outside food fee is fifty dollars but is waived for the Ultimate Party. A go-kart race add-on costs nine dollars and ninety cents per person, already included in the Ultimate Party. A late ordering fee within twenty-four hours is twenty dollars.

## Terms & Conditions

Birthday party terms and conditions at AeroSports Scarborough include free rescheduling up to one year from the booking date. Cancellations with fourteen or more days' notice receive a store gift card for the refund amount, while cancellations within fourteen days result in no refund. Package changes must be made fourteen or more days in advance and are subject to availability. Waivers are required for all jumpers; those under eighteen must have their waivers signed by a parent or guardian and remain valid for one year. Room supplies such as plates, cups, and tablecloths are included. The jumping area is shared with other patrons, but the party room is private. Prohibited items include piñatas, confetti, and silly string, which incur a forty-dollar cleaning charge if used. Guests should advise of any food allergies or sensitivities in advance. All guests should arrive twenty minutes early for check-in. AeroSports Scarborough is not responsible for lost or stolen items.
