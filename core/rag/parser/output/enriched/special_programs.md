Toddler Time is designed for children aged zero to five years old only. One parent or guardian can jump free with the toddler, providing a safe environment for little ones to explore trampolines without bigger kids around. This program is available on Saturday mornings from nine o'clock in the morning to ten o'clock in the morning and costs fifteen dollars and ninety cents.

Glow Night offers glow-in-the-dark experiences at all times, featuring neon lights and glow effects that are always active. Unlike other locations, there's no need for white or neon shirts. This program is available every day during regular hours, with the time coinciding with those regular hours, and it costs the same as regular admission.

The Special Needs Program provides one hundred and twenty minutes of playtime without including go-kart races. One support worker can be admitted free with a waiver. The program is open to all guests with special needs and is available during all operating hours at any time during park hours. The price for this program is nineteen dollars and ninety cents.
