Go karting offers various racing experiences on both the Main Track and Mini Track, each with specific pricing and height requirements for participants. The Main Track standalone race costs nineteen dollars and ninety cents per participant. To participate in this race, drivers must be at least fifty-two inches tall. This option allows up to ten laps and is available for guests who are not purchasing a jump pass.

For those interested in an add-on race on the Main Track, the price is nine dollars and ninety cents. Similar to the standalone race, participants must also meet the minimum height requirement of fifty-two inches. The add-on race permits up to ten laps and is intended for guests who have already purchased a jump pass.

On the Mini Track, the standalone race is priced at nine dollars and ninety cents per participant. Drivers need to be at least thirty-six inches tall to participate in this race. Like its Main Track counterpart, it allows up to ten laps and is available for guests not purchasing a jump pass.

For an add-on experience on the Mini Track, participants can join for four dollars and ninety cents. The minimum height requirement remains at thirty-six inches. This option also provides up to ten laps and is designed for guests who have already purchased a jump pass.
