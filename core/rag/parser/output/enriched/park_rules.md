All jumpers must wear AeroSports Socks on all park attractions, which cost three dollars and ninety-nine cents each and are reusable. Adults or individuals staying to watch their kids or supervise are counted as spectators and do not need to pay for entry. Toddlers under two years old must be accompanied by a parent who has purchased an adult jump ticket for ages six and up.

Trampolines have a three hundred pound weight limit. All jumpers must have a completed liability waiver on file, and anyone under eighteen must have the waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian. The waiver can be filled out online at waiver.roller.app/aerosportsscarborough or completed on site.

Anyone eighteen years old or older can sign their own waiver with a valid photo ID that includes a picture and date of birth. All purchases are final and non-refundable, but rescheduling is free of charge if done twenty-four hours or more before the reservation date; this does not apply to birthday party schedule changes. All prices shown include HST.

Closed-toed shoes are required for Go Karting. The Main Track requires jumpers to be at least fifty-four inches tall, while the Mini Track requires a minimum height of thirty-six inches. Stand-alone Go Kart tickets are available for guests who are not purchasing a jump pass, whereas Go Kart Add-Ons are for those who have already purchased a jump pass.

Children under ten years old cannot be dropped off without an adult present, and all children under eighteen need a parent or legal guardian to sign the waiver. Outside food is not allowed in the park except with certain birthday party packages; however, The Snack Shack sells pizza, slushies, candy, drinks, and other treats.

Complimentary Wi-Fi is available for parents throughout the facility, which has plenty of seating. We hire individuals who are sixteen years old or older. Gift cards can be purchased at the park or online, and jump cards never expire; they can be used by different jumpers.
