Welcome to AeroSports Scarborough! We have a variety of rules in place to ensure everyone has a safe and enjoyable experience while visiting our park. These guidelines cover everything from attire requirements for specific activities, such as trampolines and go-karting, to policies on refunds and food. Let's walk through the key rules so you know what to expect.

Firstly, all jumpers are required to wear AeroSports Socks when using any of our park attractions. These socks cost three dollars and ninety-nine cents each and are reusable, making them a great investment for frequent visitors. This rule ensures safety on all trampoline surfaces.

For those who will be watching their children or supervising without participating in the activities themselves, you're considered spectators and do not need to pay an entry fee. This allows parents or guardians to stay with their kids at no additional cost.

If your toddler is under two years old, they must have a parent present who has purchased an adult jump ticket for ages six and up. This ensures that young children are always supervised by someone who has access to the park's attractions.

Our trampolines have a weight limit of three hundred pounds. This safety measure helps prevent accidents and ensures the equipment is used within its designed capacity.

All jumpers must have a completed liability waiver on file before participating in any activities. Anyone under eighteen years old needs this waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian. You can fill out the waiver online at our website or complete it on-site when you arrive.

For individuals who are eighteen years of age or older, they can sign their own waiver as long as they provide a valid photo ID that includes both a picture and date of birth. This helps us verify your identity and ensure all waivers are properly documented.

Regarding purchases, please note that all transactions are final and non-refundable. However, we do offer free rescheduling for reservations made twenty-four hours or more before the scheduled date. Please be aware that this policy does not apply to birthday party schedule changes.

All prices displayed include Harmonized Sales Tax (HST), so there won't be any surprises when you make a purchase.

For those interested in go-karting, please remember that closed-toed shoes are required for safety reasons. Additionally, height restrictions apply: the Main Track requires participants to be at least fifty-four inches tall, while the Mini Track has a minimum height requirement of thirty-six inches.

Go-Kart tickets come in two types: Stand-Alone and Add-Ons. Stand-Alone Go-Kart tickets are intended for guests who are not purchasing a jump pass, whereas Go-Kart Add-Ons are available to those who have already bought a jump pass.

Children under ten years old cannot be left unattended at the park; they must always be with an adult. Furthermore, all children under eighteen need a parent or legal guardian to sign their liability waiver before participating in any activities.

Regarding food, outside items are not permitted within the park, except for certain birthday party packages that may allow them. However, our Snack Shack offers a variety of treats including pizza, slushies, candy, drinks, and more.

We provide complimentary Wi-Fi throughout the facility to ensure parents can stay connected while supervising their children. There's plenty of seating available so you can relax comfortably.

If you're considering employment with us, we hire individuals who are sixteen years old or older. This provides an opportunity for teens looking for part-time work in a fun environment.

Lastly, gift cards are available for purchase both at the park and online. Our jump cards never expire and can be used by different jumpers, making them perfect gifts for anyone who loves trampolines!

If you have any more questions or need further clarification on our rules, feel free to ask. We're here to help make your visit as enjoyable as possible!
