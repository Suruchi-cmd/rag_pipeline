# Group Bookings at AeroSports Scarborough

AeroSports Scarborough offers group booking options for jumpers, with specific terms and pricing. A minimum of fifteen jumpers is required to qualify for a group booking. Groups can benefit from a discount code named "GROUPBOOKING25," which provides a twenty-five percent reduction in price when there are fifteen or more participants.

When making a reservation, a deposit amounting to twenty-five percent of the total cost must be paid at the time of booking. For bookings that include one hundred and twenty minutes of jump time per jumper, the discounted rate is twenty-five dollars and forty-seven cents per person. This price reflects a twenty-five percent discount on the original rate of thirty-three dollars and eighty-nine cents, which includes ninety minutes of jumping for twenty-nine dollars and ninety cents plus an additional three dollars and ninety-nine cents for Aero Socks.

The pricing remains consistent regardless of whether the booking is made for a weekday or a weekend. The jump time package also comes with Aero Socks included in the price. However, go-karting activities are not part of the group rate and can be added separately at an additional cost.

# Corporate & Team Parties

AeroSports Scarborough provides options for corporate and team parties that serve as excellent venues for team-building exercises, milestone celebrations, or employee rewards. The energetic environment includes trampoline courts and obstacle courses among other attractions. To arrange a corporate booking, interested parties can call two-eight-nine-four-five-four-five-five-five-five or send an email to events.scb@aerosportsparks.ca. Customizable packages are available for these events.

# School and Field Trips

Schools, church groups, sports teams, and youth organizations have the opportunity to book school and field trips at AeroSports Scarborough. These bookings require a minimum of fifteen participants and focus on promoting active play and teamwork in a safe environment. Interested parties can make arrangements by calling two-eight-nine-four-five-four-five-five-five-five or emailing events.scb@aerosportsparks.ca. The facilities available for these trips include trampoline courts, obstacle courses, and more.

# Fundraising Events

Organizations looking to host fundraising events at AeroSports Scarborough will earn twenty percent of the night's sales from jump time. This option is suitable for schools, teams, troops, and community organizations aiming to raise funds. To book a fundraiser event, interested parties can call two-eight-nine-four-five-four-five-five-five-five or email events.scb@aerosportsparks.ca. Customizable packages are available with promotional support included.

# Facility Rentals

AeroSports Scarborough offers facility rentals for exclusive access to the entire venue. The rental cost is four thousand dollars for sixty minutes on weekdays, from Monday to Thursday, and five thousand dollars for sixty minutes during weekends, from Friday to Sunday. These rentals accommodate up to two hundred and twenty jumpers with private access and dedicated staff present on-site.

Included in the facility rental are jump socks, all party rooms, and cutlery. However, food and catering services incur additional costs. Unlimited go-karting is included as part of the facility rental package.

# Room Rentals

For smaller gatherings, AeroSports Scarborough provides room rentals with various options available. A standard party room can accommodate between twenty-five to thirty people for forty-five minutes at a cost of one hundred dollars per session. Alternatively, the Birds Eye Party Arena can hold up to eighty people and is available for two hundred dollars per forty-five-minute session.
