# Voice Call Scripts

At AeroSports Scarborough, we provide a variety of entertainment options including jump passes, birthday parties, go-karting, group bookings, promotions, camps, and more. This page covers the voice call scripts used by our team to assist customers with inquiries about these offerings.

## Greeting

> Thank you for calling AeroSports Scarborough! How can I help you today?

*Note: Use warm, upbeat tone*

## Hold

> One moment while I look that up for you.

*Note: Use when retrieving info*

## Transfer To Human

> Let me connect you with one of our team members who can help you with that. One moment please.

*Note: Use when the question is beyond what the AI can handle*

## Closing

> Thanks for calling AeroSports Scarborough! We look forward to seeing you. Have a great day!

*Note: Always end on a positive note*

## Address Response

> We are at 1120 Birchmount Road in Scarborough. The postal code is M1K 5G4.

*Note: Speak address clearly and slowly*

## Hours Response

> We are open ....

*Note: Say times clearly with AM/PM*

## Price Overview

Our Premium Jump Pass costs $24.90 for 90 minutes, while the VIP Jump Pass is priced at $29.90 for 120 minutes. Additionally, we offer an All Day Pass for $39.90 and an ultimate pass that includes go-karting, laser tag, etc., for $49.90 for 180 minutes. Please note all prices are plus tax. Aero Socks are required and cost $3.99 but they are reusable so you can bring them back next time.

*Note: Give a brief summary, offer to go deeper*

## Birthday Overview

We have three birthday party packages available. The Premium Party starts at $39.90 per jumper, offering 75 minutes of jump time and 45 minutes in a private party room. The VIP Party is priced at $49.90 per jumper, providing 90 minutes of jump time and 60 minutes of room time. Lastly, the Ultimate Party costs $59.90 per jumper, featuring 120 minutes of jump time, 60 minutes of room time, plus go-karting for every jumper. All packages include pizza and drinks with a minimum requirement of 10 jumpers.

*Note: Pause between packages for clarity*

## Gokart Overview

Go-karting is available on two tracks at AeroSports Scarborough. The main track is suitable for riders who are 54 inches or taller, costing $19.90 for a standalone race or $9.90 as an add-on if you already have a jump pass. The mini track accommodates riders 36 inches or taller and costs $9.90 standalone or $4.90 as an add-on. Each race can last up to 10 laps. Please remember that closed-toed shoes are required for go-karting.

*Note: Mention height requirements clearly*

## Booking Redirect

You can book online at our website aerosportsparks.ca, or I can assist you with the booking process right now. Would you like to proceed?

*Note: Guide toward booking*

## Waiver Info

A signed waiver is required for everyone entering the park, including non-jumpers. You can fill it out online ahead of time at our website to save time during check-in. For anyone under 18, a parent or legal guardian must sign the waiver.

*Note: Emphasize online waiver to reduce wait times*

## Group Booking

For groups of 15 or more, we offer a 25% discount with the code GROUPBOOKING25. This reduces the price to approximately $25.47 per person for 120 minutes, including socks. Would you like to book a group visit?

*Note: Emphasize the savings*

## Camp Overview

AeroCamp is available for kids aged 4 to 12. The full-day camp runs from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM, costing $69.90 per day or $299.90 for a full week. Half-day options are also available for $39.90. Activities include trampolines, games, challenges, crafts, and group activities. Early drop-off is available from 7:30 AM and late pick-up past 6 PM for an additional $10 each.

*Note: Mention current camp availability if applicable*
