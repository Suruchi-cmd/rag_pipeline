# Attractions

## Freestyle / Open Jump

At AeroSports Scarborough, the Freestyle/Open Jump attraction features wall-to-wall trampolines designed for jumping, flipping, and freestyling. This main trampoline area consists of interconnected trampolines that provide endless bouncing fun.

- **Age/Height Requirements:** Suitable for all ages. Children who can walk are welcome to jump.
- **Included in Jump Pass:** Yes
- **Pricing:** The attraction is included with any jump pass.

## Rock Walls

The Rock Walls attraction offers indoor rock climbing walls at various difficulty levels, allowing visitors to test their strength and push their limits as they climb to the top.

- **Age/Height Requirements:** Suitable for all ages.
- **Included in Jump Pass:** Yes
- **Pricing:** The attraction is included with any jump pass.

## Go Karting

Indoor go kart racing at AeroSports Scarborough features two tracks: a main track designed for taller riders and a Mini Track suitable for younger children. Participants can enjoy up to ten laps per race on electric karts.

- **Age/Height Requirements:** Main Track requires participants to be fifty-two inches tall. The Mini Track is for those who are thirty-six inches tall. Closed-toed shoes are required.
- **Included in Jump Pass:** No
- **Pricing:** This attraction requires a separate ticket or can be added as an add-on. For pricing details, refer to the Go Karting sheet.

## 360 Bicycle

The 360 Bicycle is a pedal-powered thrill ride where participants cycle through a full three hundred sixty-degree loop in the air, offering a unique experience not commonly found at most parks.
