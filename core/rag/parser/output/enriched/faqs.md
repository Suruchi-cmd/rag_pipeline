# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Information

### Jumping and Passes

- **Jump Duration**: How long does a Premium pass last?
  - A Premium pass is valid for 1 hour and 30 minutes.
  
- **Maximizing Jump Time**:
  - For extended jumping, consider an ALL day pass which offers 5 hours of jump time. This can be more beneficial than multiple Premium passes.

### Group Booking

- **Booking Process**: How do I book a group event?
  - You can book through our website or by phone. A 25% deposit is required to confirm your reservation.
  
- **Jumpers List Requirement**:
  - Provide the jumpers list two weeks before the booking date for pre-checking waivers and faster check-in.

- **Pricing Consistency**: Is pricing the same on weekdays and weekends?
  - Group jump pricing remains consistent throughout the week. However, facility rental rates may vary.

- **Group Jump Inclusions**:
  - Jump time and Aero socks are included. Additional options can be added at an extra cost.

- **Minimum Jumper Count**:
  - A minimum of 15 jumpers is required to qualify for group event pricing.

- **Private Rooms**:
  - Private rooms are available for rent at an additional cost.

### Facility Rental

- **Inclusions**: What's included in the facility rental?
  - Complimentary Aero socks, exclusive access (up to 220 jumpers), all party rooms with tablecloths and cutlery. Unlimited go karting is provided. Food/catering can be arranged at an additional cost.

### Birthday Parties

- **Guest Arrival**: How early should guests arrive for a birthday party?
  - Guests should arrive 20 minutes before the scheduled jump time for check-in.
  
- **Sock Inclusions**:
  - Sock inclusions vary by package: Premium (No), VIP (50% off), Ultimate (FREE socks).

- **Additional Jumpers**:
  - You can invite additional jumpers beyond the minimum at a per-jumper price. Ensure total doesn't exceed room capacity.

- **Add-ons**: What if I forget to pre-order add-ons?
  - Add-ons can be ordered within 24 hours but will incur a $20 late ordering fee.

- **Attraction Access**:
  - Attractions are shared with other patrons, though your party room is private.

### Aero Camp

- **Camp Inclusions**: What's included in Aero Camp?
  - Supervised trampoline activities, games, challenges, crafts, contests, and group activities.

- **Booking Flexibility**:
  - You can book individual days or a full week. Both pricing options are available.

- **Lunch Options**:
  - Lunch is not included but can be added for $9.90/day, or you may send a packed lunch.

### Jump Prices

- **Young Children's Passes**: If my child is 3 years old, can they use the Premium pass?
  - Yes, children aged 3 can use the Premium pass which lasts for 1 hour and 30 minutes.

## Group Booking Details

- **Cancellation/Refund Policy**:
  - Deposits are non-refundable. Rescheduling options may be available; contact our team for details.
  
- **Food and Catering Options**:
  - Food and catering can be arranged at an additional cost.

- **Discount Code**: What is the discount code for group events?
  - Use code GROUPBOOKING25 to receive a 25% discount on group rates for bookings of 15+ jumpers.

- **Go Karting Inclusion**:
  - Group bookings include 1x go kart race per jumper. Unlimited go karting is available with facility rentals.

## Birthday Party Details

- **Party Room Requests**: Can I request a specific party room?
  - We try to accommodate requests based on availability but cannot guarantee them.

- **Parental Waiting Area**:
  - Parents can relax in our viewing area or common spaces while their children enjoy the party.

- **Decorations Policy**:
  - You may bring outside decorations as long as they comply with park policies. A $40 cleaning charge applies if used.

- **Check-in Process**: What is the check-in process for party guests?
  - Staff will assist with check-in, and ensure all waivers are completed beforehand. Wristbands will be provided to guests.

- **Dietary Accommodations**:
  - Dietary restrictions can be accommodated with advance notice. Contact us to discuss options.

- **Late Parties**: What if my party runs late?
  - Parties must adhere to the scheduled time, and overages may result in additional fees.

- **Birthday Child Counting**:
  - The birthday child is included in the jumper count for your package.

- **Invitations**:
  - Digital invitations are included with every package. Physical invitations can be provided at $0.75 each.

## Aero Camp Details

- **Registration**: How do I register my child?
  - Register online or call the park. Early registration is recommended as spots fill up quickly.

- **Camp Requirements**:
  - Comfortable activewear, packed lunch/snacks (if not buying lunch combo), and a refillable water bottle are required for campers.

- **Supervision**: Is there supervision during camp?
  - Yes, trained staff supervise at all times.

- **Drop-off/Pick-up Flexibility**:
  - Early drop-offs from 7:30 AM or late pick-ups past 6 PM can typically be arranged with short notice. Notify the camp team as soon as possible.

- **Age Requirement**: What is the age requirement for Aero Camp?
  - Campers must be between 4 and 12 years old.

- **Waivers**:
  - A signed waiver is required before attending camp, which can be completed online or at the park.

- **Camp Timing**:
  - PA Day Camps are offered on all PA days per Catholic and Public Elementary School Boards. Summer Camp runs during specific summer weeks, with dates announced closer to start. March Break Camp occurs during official March Break for all elementary and secondary students, while Winter Camp is held during the winter holiday period.

- **Parental Presence**: Can parents stay during camp?
  - While drop-off is encouraged for a smooth experience, let us know if your child needs settling-in support.

- **Extended Hours**:
  - Supervised free play or quiet activities are available. Early drop-offs start at 7:30 AM and late pick-ups can be arranged past 6 PM (prices vary).

- **Lost and Found**: Is there a lost and found?
  - Yes, please label all personal items.

- **Electronic Devices**:
  - Electronic devices should ideally be left at home to avoid distractions or damage.

- **Rest Periods**:
  - Short breaks for rest, snacks, and lunch are included. Quiet activities like crafts may also be offered during these times.

- **Handling Illness**: What if my child feels unwell?
  - We will notify you immediately. Trained staff handle minor first aid, and emergency contacts should be provided at registration.

- **Behavioral Management**:
  - Staff manage conflicts calmly and parents are informed if further action is needed.

- **Lunch Combo Details**: What is included in the lunch combo?
  - The lunch combo includes kid-friendly options such as pizza, a drink, and a snack.

- **Late Arrival Policy**: Can my child join if they miss the first day?
  - Yes, late arrivals are welcome if space is available.
