# Frequently Asked Questions

At AeroSports Scarborough, we offer a variety of family entertainment options including trampolines, go-karting, and more. This page covers frequently asked questions about general park information, specific attractions like go-karting, group bookings, birthday parties, Aero Camp details, and jump pass pricing.

## General

**Q: Are AeroSports jump socks required?**

A: Yes, AeroSports socks are required at all locations. Jump socks are available for $3.99 + tax. They are reusable, so keep yours for future visits!

**Q: Is there a weight limit for the trampolines?**

A: Our trampolines have a 300-pound weight limit.

**Q: Are waivers required? Can they be filled out online?**

A: Waivers are required for every patron. They can be completed online to speed up check-in.

**Q: What if my child gets injured?**

A: Trained staff respond on-site. Parents will be notified immediately, and emergency protocols are in place for serious injuries.

**Q: Can I tour the facility before booking?**

A: Yes! Contact us to arrange a visit.

**Q: Are there options for non-jumpers?**

A: Non-jumpers can enjoy the party room and food but not attractions. They do not count toward the minimum jumper requirement.

**Q: What if my child gets injured during camp?**

A: We will notify you immediately. Trained staff handle minor first-aid, and emergency contacts are provided at registration.

## Jump Prices

**Q: If my child is 3 years old, can they use the Premium pass, and how long does it last?**

A: Yes, a child aged 3 can use the Premium pass, which lasts for 1 hour and 30 minutes.

**Q: What if I want to maximize jumping time for my kids—should I opt for the VIP pass or multiple Premium passes?**

A: You can get an ALL day pass that provides you with 5 hours of jump time.

## Birthday Party

**Q: How early should guests arrive?**

A: All guests should arrive 20 minutes before the scheduled jump time for check-in.

**Q: Are socks included in the party package?**

A: Sock inclusions vary by package. Premium packages do not include socks, VIP offers them at 50% off, and Ultimate includes free socks.

**Q: Can I invite additional jumpers beyond the minimum?**

A: Yes, additional jumpers can be added at the per-jumper price. Ensure the total does not exceed room capacity.

**Q: What if some guests don't show up?**

A: The minimum jumper count for your package is still charged. Additional fees apply for extra arrivals.

## Group Booking

**Q: Is a jumpers list required?**

A: Yes, provide the jumpers list two weeks before the booking date so we can pre-check waivers and speed up check-in.

**Q: What's included in the group jump?**

A: Jump time and Aero socks are included. Add-on options are available at extra cost.

**Q: Are food and catering options available?**

A: Yes, food and catering can be arranged at additional cost.

## Aero Camp

**Q: What's included in Aero Camp?**

A: Supervised trampoline activities, games, challenges, crafts, contests, and group activities are included.

**Q: Is lunch included?**

A: Lunch is not included but can be added for $9.90/day. You can also send a packed lunch.

**Q: How do I register my child?**

A: Register online or call the park. Spots fill up quickly, so early registration is recommended.

## Go-Karting

**Q: Is go-karting included in group bookings?**

A: Yes! One go-kart race per jumper for group bookings. Unlimited go-karting is available for facility rentals.

## General Information

**Q: What if my party runs late?**

A: Parties must follow the scheduled time. Overages may result in additional fees.

**Q: Can I bring outside decorations?**

A: Yes, as long as they comply with policies. No confetti, piñatas, or silly string. A $40 cleaning charge applies if used.

**Q: What is the cancellation/rescheduling policy?**

A: Reschedule within 1 year at no charge. Cancel 14+ days in advance for a store gift card. Within 14 days, there is no refund.

## Additional Information

**Q: Can I split the party cost?**

A: Yes, payments can be split. Let us know in advance.

**Q: Do you help with setup and cleanup?**

A: Yes, staff assists with setup and handles cleanup.

**Q: Are electronic devices allowed during camp?**

A: We recommend leaving them at home to avoid distractions or damage.

**Q: When are PA Day Camps offered?**

A: Available on all PA days per Catholic and Public Elementary School Boards. Check the website or call for details.

This FAQ aims to provide comprehensive information about AeroSports Scarborough's offerings, ensuring a smooth experience for families visiting our park.
