# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Information

### Jumping and Socks
- **Jump Time**: Jumpers receive one hour and thirty minutes of jumping time with a Premium Pass. For extended play, consider the ALL DAY pass offering five hours.
- **Socks Inclusion**: Aero socks are included in all jump passes.

### Add-Ons
- **Add-On Options**: Additional activities like go-karting or laser tag can be added to your visit for an extra fee.
- **Late Ordering Fee**: If add-ons aren't pre-ordered, a $20 late ordering fee applies. Pre-order within 24 hours of arrival to avoid this charge.

### Waivers
- **Waiver Requirement**: A signed waiver is mandatory before participating in any activities. Complete waivers online or at the park upon arrival.

## Birthday Parties

### Party Details
- **Socks Inclusion by Package**:
  - Premium: No socks included.
  - VIP: Socks available at a 50% discount.
  - Ultimate: Free socks provided.
  
- **Additional Jumpers**: You can add more jumpers beyond the minimum required, charged per additional jumper. Ensure total does not exceed room capacity.

### Invitations and Decorations
- **Invitation Options**:
  - Digital invitations are included in every package.
  - Physical invitations available at $0.75 each.
  
- **Decorations**: Bring outside decorations if they comply with park policies. A cleaning fee of $40 applies for certain items like confetti, piñatas, or silly string.

### Party Management
- **Check-In Process**: Staff assists with check-in; ensure all waivers are completed beforehand and wristbands provided.
  
- **Dietary Restrictions**: Accommodations can be made with advance notice. Contact the park to discuss options.

## Group Bookings

### Booking Details
- **Deposit Requirement**: A 25% deposit is required when booking a group event, confirmed within the holding period to secure your reservation.
  
- **Jumpers List**: Provide a list of jumpers two weeks before the event for pre-checking waivers and expedited check-in.

### Pricing and Inclusions
- **Pricing Consistency**: Group pricing remains consistent across weekdays and weekends. Facility rental rates vary.
  
- **Included Features**:
  - Jump time and Aero socks are included in group bookings.
  - Additional add-ons available at extra cost.

## Camps

### Registration and Requirements
- **Registration Process**: Register online or by calling the park. Early registration is recommended as spots fill up quickly.
  
- **Waiver Requirement**: A signed waiver is necessary for camp participation, which can be completed online or at the park.

### Schedule and Supervision
- **Camp Age Range**: Camps are designed for children aged 4 to 12 years old.
  
- **Supervision**: Trained staff supervise all activities throughout the camp duration.

## Additional Information

### Go-Karting Details
- **Age Requirement**: Participants must be at least five years old and meet a minimum height requirement of 48 inches.
  
- **Cost**: A standalone go-kart race costs $19.90, with discounts available for those holding jump passes.

### Facility Rental
- **Inclusions**:
  - Complimentary Aero socks.
  - Exclusive facility access (up to 220 jumpers).
  - Access to all party rooms and amenities like tablecloths and cutlery.
  
- **Additional Services**: Food and catering services are available at an additional cost.

### Cancellation Policy
- **Deposits**: Deposits for bookings are non-refundable.
  
- **Rescheduling Options**: Rescheduling may be possible; contact the team for details. 

This FAQ aims to provide comprehensive answers to common questions, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience at our facility. For further inquiries or specific requests, please reach out directly to our customer service team.
