Welcome to Aero Camp, where your child will have a fantastic and active experience! At our camp, we offer supervised trampoline activities along with games, challenges, crafts, contests, and various group activities designed to keep kids engaged and entertained. You can choose to book individual days or opt for the full week, depending on what suits you best.

For those who prefer convenience, lunch is available at an additional cost of $9.90 per day, but if your child has specific dietary needs or preferences, feel free to send a packed lunch instead. To ensure everything runs smoothly, we recommend registering early as spots fill up quickly. You can register online or by calling the park.

On camp days, children should come dressed in comfortable activewear and bring their refillable water bottle along with any snacks if they're not opting for our lunch combo. Rest assured, trained staff will be there to supervise your child at all times, ensuring a safe and enjoyable experience.

If you need flexibility with drop-off or pick-up times, we can usually accommodate last-minute changes—just let us know as soon as possible. The camp is designed for children aged 4-12 years old, and a signed waiver is required before attending, which you can complete online or at the park upon arrival.

Our camps are scheduled around school breaks, including PA Day Camps on all PA days according to Catholic and Public Elementary School Boards, Summer Camp during specific summer weeks, March Break Camp during official March Break for elementary and secondary students, and Winter Camp during the winter holiday period. Dates for these sessions will be announced closer to their start.

We encourage parents to drop off their children to ensure a smooth experience, although we understand some may need settling-in support. If your child feels unwell or if there are any behavioral issues, our trained staff are equipped to handle these situations and will keep you informed as necessary.

For those who want to make the most of camp hours, we offer extended hours with supervised free play or quiet activities for early drop-offs starting at 7:30 AM and late pick-ups past 6 PM. Please note that prices may vary for these additional times.

Lastly, we have a lost and found area, so it's helpful if your child’s personal items are labeled. We recommend leaving electronic devices at home to avoid distractions or potential damage during camp activities.

If you have any more questions or need further details about Aero Camp, feel free to reach out!
