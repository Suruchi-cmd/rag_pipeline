Aero Camp offers a variety of camp programs with specific schedules and prices. The PA Day Camp runs from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM, costing sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents for a full day. Similarly, the Summer/Winter/March Break Camp is available at the same time, also priced at sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents per full day. For those interested in attending the entire week during these breaks, the cost is two hundred ninety-nine dollars and ninety cents. The camp also offers half-day options from 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM or from 12:30 PM to 4:30 PM, each costing thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents per day; however, the morning option is available only during summer camp. Additionally, there's a half-day week package priced at one hundred sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents.

Aero Camp provides several add-ons for an additional cost per day. A Lunch Combo can be added for nine dollars and ninety cents. For early drop-offs starting from 7:30 AM, there is a charge of ten dollars, while late pick-ups past 6 PM also incur a fee of ten dollars.

The camp has specific details to note. It caters to children aged four to twelve years old. Trained staff are present at all times for supervision. The activities include trampolines, games, challenges, crafts, contests, and group activities. Participation requires waivers that must be completed online or at the park. Registration can be done through their website or by calling the park directly.
