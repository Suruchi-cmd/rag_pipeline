## Aero Camp Programs Overview

Aero Camp offers a variety of camp programs with different schedules and pricing options. The PA Day Camp runs from eight-thirty in the morning to four-thirty in the afternoon, costing sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents for a full day. Similarly, the Summer, Winter, and March Break Camps follow the same schedule and price for a full day.

For those interested in attending the entire week of camp during these breaks, the program runs from eight-thirty in the morning to four-thirty in the afternoon each day, with a total cost of two hundred ninety-nine dollars and ninety cents. There are also half-day options available: one session is from eight-thirty in the morning to twelve-thirty in the afternoon, and another from twelve-thirty in the afternoon to four-thirty in the afternoon, both costing thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents per day. Additionally, a half-day week option is offered at one hundred sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents.

## Aero Camp Add-Ons

Aero Camp provides several add-ons available on a daily basis. The Lunch Combo can be added for nine dollars and ninety cents. For those needing to drop off campers early, starting from seven-thirty in the morning, there is an additional charge of ten dollars. Similarly, if pick-up extends past six o'clock in the evening, it incurs another ten-dollar fee.

## Aero Camp Details

Aero Camp caters to children aged four to twelve years old and ensures trained staff are present at all times for supervision. The camp features a range of activities including trampolines, games, challenges, crafts, contests, and group activities. Participation requires completing waivers, which can be done online or directly at the park.

Registration for Aero Camp is available through their website or by calling the park directly to secure a spot in any of the offered programs.
