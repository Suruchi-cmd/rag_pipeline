At AeroSports Scarborough, we offer a variety of camp programs designed to suit different schedules and preferences. Our camps include options like the PA Day Camp and Summer/Winter/March Break Camps, which are available in full day, half day, and weekly formats. These programs cater to children aged four to twelve years old and feature engaging activities such as trampolines, games, crafts, and contests under the supervision of trained staff.

The PA Day Camp runs from eight thirty ante meridiem to four thirty post meridiem at a price of sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents for the full day. Similarly, our Summer/Winter/March Break Camps offer flexible scheduling options: a full day program also costs sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents, while a full week is priced at two hundred ninety-nine dollars and ninety cents. For those who prefer shorter durations, we have half-day options available from eight thirty ante meridiem to twelve thirty post meridiem or from twelve thirty post meridiem to four thirty post meridiem, each costing thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents. Additionally, there is a half-day week option priced at one hundred sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents.

We also provide several add-ons for our camp programs. For an additional nine dollars and ninety cents per day, you can opt for the Lunch Combo, which includes meals during the camp hours. If your schedule requires early drop-off or late pick-up, we offer these services as well: dropping off your child from seven thirty ante meridiem costs ten dollars, and picking them up past six post meridiem also incurs a fee of ten dollars.

To ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants, our camps have specific requirements. Children must be between the ages of four and twelve years old to join. All activities are supervised by trained staff at all times, ensuring a secure environment. The camp features a variety of fun activities including trampolines, games, challenges, crafts, contests, and group activities designed to keep children engaged throughout their stay.

Before attending, it's important for parents or guardians to complete the necessary waivers. These can be filled out online or at the park upon arrival. Registration is straightforward; you can sign up through our website or by calling the park directly.
