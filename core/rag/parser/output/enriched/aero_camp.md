# Aero Camp

At AeroSports Scarborough, we offer a variety of camp programs designed to provide fun and engaging experiences for children. This page covers details about our camp schedules, pricing options, add-ons, age requirements, supervision, activities, waivers, and registration processes.

## Camp Programs

The PA Day Camp runs from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM with a full day price of $69.90. The Summer/Winter/March Break Camp also operates from 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM at the same full-day rate of $69.90. Additionally, this camp offers a full week option for $299.90.

For half-day options during the Summer/Winter/March Break Camp, there are two time slots available: an AM session from 8:30 AM to 12:30 PM and a PM session from 12:30 PM to 4:30 PM, each costing $39.90. The summer camp specifically offers these half-day options. There is also a Half Day Week option priced at $169.90.

## Add-Ons (Per Day)

- Lunch Combo costs $9.90 per day.
- Early Drop Off from 7:30 AM incurs an additional charge of $10.00.
- Late Pick Up past 6 PM also adds $10.00 to the daily cost.

## Camp Details

The camp is suitable for children aged 4-12 years old and ensures trained staff supervision at all times. Activities include trampoline sessions, games, challenges, crafts, contests, and group activities. Waivers are required and can be completed online or at the park. Registration can be done via the website or by calling the park directly.
