## Aero Camp Overview

Aero Camp offers a variety of camp programs with different schedules and pricing options. The age requirement for participants is four to twelve years old, and trained staff supervise all activities at all times.

### Camp Programs Details

- **PA Day Camp** runs from 8:30 Ante Meridiem to 4:30 Post Meridiem and costs sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents for a full day.
  
- **Summer/Winter/March Break Camp** also operates from 8:30 Ante Meridiem to 4:30 Post Meridiem, with the same full-day price of sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents.

- For those interested in an extended stay, the Summer/Winter/March Break Camp offers a full week option at two hundred ninety-nine dollars and ninety cents.

- Half-day options are available from 8:30 Ante Meridiem to 12:30 Post Meridiem or from 12:30 Post Meridiem to 4:30 Post Meridiem, each costing thirty-nine dollars and ninety cents. The morning session is an option only during the summer camp.

- Additionally, a half-day week program is available for one hundred sixty-nine dollars and ninety cents.

### Add-Ons

Aero Camp provides several add-ons that can be purchased per day:

- A **Lunch Combo** is available for nine dollars and ninety cents.
  
- An **Early Drop Off** service from 7:30 Ante Meridiem costs ten dollars.
  
- For those needing a **Late Pick Up** past 6 Post Meridiem, the charge is also ten dollars.

### Camp Details

Participants can enjoy various activities including trampolines, games, challenges, crafts, contests, and group activities. It's important to note that waivers are required for participation; these can be completed online or at the park itself. Registration for Aero Camp can be done through their website or by calling the park directly.
